## 1) Google Play Console Listing Copy

### App Name
PointChat

### Short Description (max 80 chars)
Real-time chat with groups, voice notes, files, location, and AI bots.

### Full Description
PointChat is a fast, modern messaging app for direct messages, group chats, and AI-powered conversations.

Connect with Google Sign-In, start 1:1 chats, create groups, and stay in sync with real-time updates.

What you can do in PointChat:
- Manage all your chats from one command-driven OneScreen workspace so you stay focused and distraction-free.
- Direct messages and group chats with live updates
- Share photos, files, voice notes, and  location pins
- Create and chat with custom AI bots
- Use built-in AI chat in your private thread
- Control seen/read receipts and notification behavior
- Lock the app and private chats with biometric unlock or PIN
- Personalize your profile with photo and status

PointChat is designed for everyday communication with extra privacy controls and optional AI features.

Notes:
- AI features require an active AI subscription.
- Subscription purchases are handled through Google Play.

## 3) Privacy Policy (Copy/Paste)

Title: Privacy Policy for PointChat

Effective date: 2026-04-09

PointChat ("we", "us", "our") provides messaging, group communication, and optional AI features. This Privacy Policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and your choices.

### 1. Information We Collect

We collect the following categories of information when you use PointChat:

1. Account and profile information
- Google account sign-in data provided through authentication (such as account ID, name, and email).
- Profile information you set in the app, such as display name, profile photo, and status.

2. Messaging and communication data
- Message content you send and receive, including text messages, image messages, file messages, voice messages, and location messages.
- Message metadata such as sender/recipient identifiers, timestamps, message type, unread/read state, and read-receipt state.
- Group data, including group name, group photo, membership, pending invitations, and admin roles.

3. AI-related data (optional features)
- Prompts and related context you send to AI features.
- AI-generated responses returned to you.
- Custom bot configuration data (for example bot name and instructions) when you create or edit bots.

4. Subscription and purchase data
- Subscription status/entitlement information used to enable AI access.
- Purchase processing data handled by app stores and subscription infrastructure.

5. Device/local app data
- Local preferences such as app lock settings, chat lock settings, and local PIN preference.
- Cached chat/message data stored locally to improve app performance.

6. Presence and activity data
- Online status and last-seen timestamps used to support presence features.

### 2. Permissions We Request

Depending on your device and usage, PointChat may request:
- Microphone permission: to record and send voice messages.
- Photos/files/storage access: to select and send media/files and update profile/group photos.
- Location permission: to share your location in chat when you choose to do so.
- Notification permission: to show message and invite notifications.
- Biometric/Face ID permission: to unlock app/chat privacy features.

We request these permissions only to provide related app functionality.

### 3. How We Use Information

We use your information to:
- Create and manage your account and profile.
- Deliver messaging, group, media-sharing, and presence features.
- Provide read receipts, seen controls, and notifications.
- Process and enforce AI subscription access.
- Generate AI responses when you use AI features.
- Improve reliability, security, and performance of the app.

### 4. How We Share Information

We share data with service providers only as needed to run PointChat features:

1. Appwrite (backend infrastructure)
- Used for authentication, database storage, file storage, and realtime updates.

2. Google Generative AI (Gemini) for AI features
- AI prompts and system instructions are sent through our cloud function to generate AI responses.

3. RevenueCat and app stores (Google Play / Apple App Store)
- Used to validate and manage subscription entitlements and purchases.

We do not sell your personal information.

### 5. Data Retention

We keep data for as long as needed to provide the service, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce agreements.

In-app actions currently available include deleting messages/chats/groups. If you need account-level deletion assistance, contact us using the developer contact email listed on the PointChat app listing.

### 6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

Biometric data itself is handled by your device operating system and is not stored by PointChat.

### 7. Children

PointChat is not intended for children under the age required by your local law to consent to data processing.

### 8. International Data Processing

Your information may be processed in countries other than your own, depending on where our service providers operate.

### 9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will post the updated version with a new effective date.

### 10. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the developer contact information listed on the PointChat store listing.
