PointChat Account Deletion

Use this page to request permanent deletion of your PointChat account.

Delete your account in the app:
  • Open PointChat.
  • Go to Settings.
  • Tap Delete account.
  • Type DELETE MY ACCOUNT and confirm.
What is deleted:
  • Account data and sign-in access.
  • Associated PointChat account data.
What may be retained:
  • Security and backup logs for up to 90 days.
  • Store billing records required by legal/accounting obligations.
