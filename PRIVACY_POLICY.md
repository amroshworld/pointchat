# Privacy Policy for PointChat

**Last updated:** April 9, 2026

Thank you for choosing to be part of our community at PointChat ("we", "us", "our"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. 

This Privacy Policy explains how our app, **PointChat**, collects, uses, and shares information about you when you use our mobile application.

## 1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information when you use our application:

### a) Personal Information Provided by You
- **Account Data:** When you sign in (e.g., via Google Sign-In or other authentication), we receive basic profile information such as your name, email address, and profile picture.
- **Communications:** Any messages, text, or media you send through our chat system.

### b) Permissions and Device Features
To provide our core chat functionalities, PointChat requests access to certain device features:
- **Microphone (RECORD_AUDIO):** We require access to your device's microphone strictly so that you can record and send voice messages to other users within the chat. We do not listen to or record your background conversations.
- **Camera & Photo Library:** We request access so you can take photos, record videos, or select media from your gallery to share in your chats and update your profile picture.
- **Storage/Files:** We require storage access to save media files locally on your device or upload documents inside your chats.
- **Location:** If you choose to share your location with other users in the chat, we will collect and process your location data strictly for this feature.

## 2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for the following purposes:
- To facilitate account creation and the logon process.
- To provide, operate, and maintain the chat functionalities.
- To transmit your text, voice, and media messages to your intended recipients.
- To notify you about new messages via push notifications.

## 3. Third-Party Services

Our app utilizes third-party services that may collect information used to identify you. 
The services we use include:
- **Appwrite:** Used for our backend database, secure message storage, and user authentication.
- **Google Sign-In:** Used to securely authenticate your account.
- **Google Play Services:** Fundamental services required for Android applications.

## 4. Data Security

We value your trust in providing us your Personal Information, thus we are striving to use commercially acceptable means of protecting it. However, remember that no method of transmission over the internet, or method of electronic storage is 100% secure and reliable, and we cannot guarantee its absolute security.

## 5. Data Retention & Deletion

We keep your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide you services. You can delete your account and associated data through the app settings or by contacting us directly. 

## 6. Children's Privacy

These Services do not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13. In the case we discover that a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, we immediately delete this from our servers.

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Thus, you are advised to review this page periodically for any changes. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
